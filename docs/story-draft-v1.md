# Story Draft v1 — for batch approval (D14, D16)

Game: **KEYBLASTER** (D65).

## How to read this

Each chapter has five parts, and each part maps to a game surface:

- **Briefing** — the story page the player reads before flight (screen 1). Grades 2–5 vocabulary. This is the source of every asteroid word in the stage.
- **Asteroid pool** — the content words pulled from the briefing. The engine picks from these (plus a few sight words from the allowlist for filler).
- **Pre-flight line** — the narrative startup sequence (D51). On a new profile this doubles as calibration; on returning profiles it's a 5-second ritual.
- **Warp sentence** — the sentence the player types in the calm break to charge the warp drive (D30). It is a sentence from the briefing, so it is built from words they just blasted. Bold marks words that appear in the asteroid pool.
- **Beacon text** — what the beacon "says" when placed. Ecliptic coordinates are computed live from the play date (D15); the pulsar line is flavor grounded in real navigation science.

Co-pilot: **Shadow**, the ship's small navigation robot (D66). Shadow speaks the pre-flight lines and the coach notes. Shadow never says "wrong."

Planet facts are real and kid-level. Anything marked *(verify)* I want to double-check before it ships.

## Notes for approval

1. **Facts to verify before shipping** *(verify)*: Jupiter "still counting moons" (true as of my knowledge, count keeps rising); Saturn "would float" (classic, density < water); Neptune winds "faster than a jet plane" (~2,000 km/h, yes); sunlight to Neptune ~4 hours (yes); Pluto's heart is nitrogen ice (Tombaugh Regio, yes); Triton orbits backward (retrograde, yes). I'll confirm each with a source in the PRD.
2. **Vocabulary level:** briefings sit around a grade 2–3 reading level so the words are readable by the low end and typeable by the high end. Asteroid pools skew short because grades 2–5 typing speed is the real limiter. The engine's word-length knob (D53) can bias toward the longer pool words for stronger players.
3. **Warp sentences are all first-sentence-of-briefing style:** short, declarative, every content word appears in the pool. Deliberate: the victory lap should feel easy (D30).
4. **Allowlist check:** every pool word is in the Fry first-1000 or is a planet/moon proper noun. Proper nouns (Phobos, Deimos, Titan, Triton, Charon) appear in briefings but are excluded from asteroid pools; kids read them, they don't have to type them.
5. **Spanish and Hindi** versions follow once English is approved (D45), as translations that preserve the pool/sentence structure, not word-for-word.
6. **Coach notes** (D33) are not scripted here; the AI generates them from misses at runtime with Shadow' voice constraints: short, warm, names the word, never says wrong.
