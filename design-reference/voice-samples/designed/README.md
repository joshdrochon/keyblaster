# Designed voices for Shadow — LISTEN AND PICK ONE

The shipped voice was **Liam** (`TX3LPaxmHKxFdv7VOQHJ`), one of ElevenLabs'
stock library voices. It was chosen from written voice descriptions by someone
who could not hear it, and it was reported as generic and over-familiar. That
is the expected outcome of picking a stock voice: they are the voices in every
ElevenLabs demo.

These nine are NOT stock. Each was generated by Voice Design from a written
character brief, so the result exists only for this game.

All nine speak the same line, so they are directly comparable:

> Belt ahead. Rocks with words on them. Type the word and the blaster does the
> rest. I will keep the lantern steady while you fly. Ready when you are, pilot.

| file | brief |
|---|---|
| `a-utility-bot-1..3` | Even, slightly clipped, faint synthetic edge. Warm in content, flat in delivery. Closest to the D66/D91 character sheet. |
| `b-brass-tin-1..3` | Thin, bright, slightly tinny — a small speaker in a metal shell. Mid-high pitch, gently mechanical cadence. |
| `c-soft-synth-1..3` | Light and airy, soft electronic shimmer. Unhurried and reassuring, never excited. |

Play them, name the winner, and all 44 lines re-render from that voice.
